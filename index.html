01

Planning & Management

Design & Décor Management

End-to-end conceptualization and execution of themes, venue styling, floral arrangements, stage design, and ambiance creation.

Consultation & Complete Planning

Strategic event planning including timelines, theme development, scheduling, and structured execution.

Budget Management

Transparent financial planning, cost optimization, and expense tracking to ensure you get the most out of your investment.

Vendor Sourcing & Management

Selection, negotiation, and coordination of trusted vendors across all categories to ensure quality and reliability.

Logistics Management

Transportation, accommodation, travel coordination, and event flow management for a seamless experience.

Hospitality & RSVP Management

Guest assistance, RSVP handling, welcome experiences, and premium hospitality services throughout the event.

02

Creative & Production

Conceptual Decoration & Venue Styling

Customized décor concepts tailored to your event theme and vision, transforming any venue into an immersive experience.

Floral Design

Professional floral styling, installations, bouquets, and statement décor elements crafted with artistry and precision.

Lighting & Sound Setup

Complete technical production including ambient and dramatic lighting, DJ systems, and full sound management.

Photography & Videography

Timeless photography and cinematic wedding films capturing every emotion, detail, and precious moment beautifully.

Pre-Wedding Shoots

Creative concept-based and destination pre-wedding shoots that tell your unique love story before the big day.

Artist & Entertainment Management

Live performers, DJs, musicians, dancers, and curated entertainment experiences tailored to your celebration.

03

Culinary & Experience

Food & Beverage Management

Designer Cakes

Bartending Services

Professional mixology, curated beverage menus, signature cocktails, and premium bar setups for your celebration.

Wedding Favours & Gifting

Personalized wedding favours, luxury hampers, and bespoke gifting solutions to express gratitude to your guests.

Stationery & Invitations

Bespoke invitations, digital invites, event branding collateral, and elegant stationery that sets the tone.

Elopement Planning

Intimate, private, and destination elopement experiences crafted with elegance, emotion, and meticulous attention to detail.
